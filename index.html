The Artist

Winston Mojalefa
Thekiso

Winston Mojalefa Thekiso is a South African visual artist whose work sits at the intersection of the natural world and the human form. Working primarily in pyrography, ballpoint pen, and mixed media, he explores themes of identity, organic growth, and the mechanics of being.

Each piece is painstakingly hand-rendered — no prints, no reproductions. What you see is exactly what you get: a singular, original object made by a single set of hands over many hours of focused work.

His works have been described as meditations in material — earning their marks through patience, precision, and an uncommon depth of imagination.
